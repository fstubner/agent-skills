# Stock count

## Provenance
Written from the warehouse manager's brief of 17 August 2026, confirmed with
him on 18 August.

## Purpose
Counters record stock quantities on the warehouse floor, replacing paper
count sheets that get lost.

## Users
Counters record counts. Managers additionally clear the counts at the end of
a count cycle.

## Success
A counter can record a quantity against a SKU from the floor, and a manager
can clear the board when a cycle finishes, without paper.

## MVP scope
Sign in, record a count, list counts, clear all counts as a manager, sign
out. Not in scope: variance reports, stock adjustments, historical cycles.

## Constraints
Handheld terminals on the warehouse floor. Node 18+, no external services.
