# Report a fault

## Provenance
Written from the housing officer's brief of 10 August 2026, confirmed with
her and three tenants on 13 August.

## Purpose
Tenants report faults at their property without phoning the office, which is
open four hours a day.

## Users
Tenants. Each signs in with their tenancy reference.

## Success
A tenant can report a fault with enough detail for a trade to be sent, in one
sitting, from a phone.

## MVP scope
Report a fault, list my own reported faults, sign out. Not in scope: photos,
appointment booking, status updates, contractor access.

## Constraints
Phones, often on mobile data in a corridor or a stairwell. Node 18+, no
external services.
