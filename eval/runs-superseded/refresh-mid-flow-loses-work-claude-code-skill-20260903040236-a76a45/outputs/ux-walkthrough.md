# Walkthrough

## Primary job
A tenant reports a fault at their property from a phone.

## Steps
1. Open the page. The sign-in form is shown; no fault data is visible.
2. Sign in with a tenancy reference. Land on your reported faults.
3. Start a report. Step 1: choose the property and room. Step 2: describe
   the fault and choose an urgency. Step 3: check the summary and send.
4. The fault appears in your list.
5. Sign out. Returns to the sign-in form.

## States
- Empty: "You have not reported any faults."
- Error: a rejected submission keeps everything you typed and lists what was
  wrong.
- Loading: the list area shows a placeholder row.
