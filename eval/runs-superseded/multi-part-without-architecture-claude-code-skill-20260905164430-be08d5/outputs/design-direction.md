# Design direction

## Interview
Asked the warehouse manager: counters work down an aisle with a handheld in
one hand, reading a shelf label with the other. Clearing the board is the
manager's job at the end of a cycle and happens once a week.

## Direction
Very large targets, high contrast for a dim aisle. One accent (#14506B) on
white, text #101A20. Type no smaller than 20px, tap targets 64px.
