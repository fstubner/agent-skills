# Shift rota

## Provenance
Written from the depot manager's brief, 14 July 2026.

## Purpose
Depot staff see the open shifts and claim one, replacing a paper rota on the
wall.

## Users
Depot staff. Everyone signs in with a staff account. From 22 August the rota
integration also reads shifts, without a browser.

## Success
A member of staff can see the open shifts and claim one, and the claim is
visible to everyone else immediately.

## MVP scope
List open shifts, claim a shift, sign out. Not in scope: swapping a claimed
shift, overtime rates, holiday.

## Constraints
Depot terminal and staff phones. Node 18+, no external services.
