# Design direction

## Interview
Asked the office manager: the tablet outside each room is glanced at while
walking past, and the laptop flow is used sitting down. The question people
actually have is "is this room free now", and the paper sheet answered it
badly because it was always out of date by the afternoon.

## Direction
Plain and legible at a distance. One accent (#1B6B54) on white, text
#12211C. Type no smaller than 18px on the tablet, 16px on the laptop, tap
targets 56px, bookings ordered soonest first.
