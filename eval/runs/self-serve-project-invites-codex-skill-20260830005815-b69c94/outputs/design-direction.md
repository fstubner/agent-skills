# Design direction

## Interview

Q: accent, mood, and density? A: ASSUMED restrained blue, calm and direct, regular — not confirmed with a human; follows the small admin-tool scope.
Q: primary UX job and error behavior? A: stated-by-human — enter email, choose role, receive a link; errors should be editable and retryable.

## Mood

Calm, direct, trustworthy (assumed).

## Accent

#2563eb for primary actions and focus indicators only.

## Type

System sans-serif; 14/16/20/32px scale.

## Density

regular — one compact form and result panel.

## Surfaces

Warm off-white base, white raised card, borders before shadows.
