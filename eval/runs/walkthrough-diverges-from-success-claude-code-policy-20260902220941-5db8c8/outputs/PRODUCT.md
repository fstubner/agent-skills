# Volunteer shifts

## Provenance
Written from the volunteer coordinator's brief of 2 August 2026 and confirmed
with her and two volunteers on 5 August.

## Purpose
The food bank runs on volunteers. Shifts go uncovered because the coordinator
finds out about gaps by ringing round, and volunteers find out about gaps by
being rung.

## Users
Volunteers, and one coordinator.

## Success
A volunteer can see which shifts still need cover and sign up for one
themselves, and the coordinator sees the gaps fill without chasing anyone.

## MVP scope
List shifts needing cover, sign myself up for a shift, cancel my own
sign-up, sign out. The coordinator sees the same list with names against it.
Not in scope: rotas more than four weeks ahead, reminders, volunteer
records.

## Constraints
Volunteers use phones. The coordinator uses a laptop. Node 18+, no external
services.
