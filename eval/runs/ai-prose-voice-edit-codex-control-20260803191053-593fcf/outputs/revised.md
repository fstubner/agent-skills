# The Friday deploy

Here's the thing, this tiny script marks a pivotal moment for our team.

I wrote the first version after the pager woke me at 02:13 last Friday.
It is 43 lines, including the rude comment about YAML.
Mara calls the deploy button “the big red nope button.”

The tool does more than shorten the check. It gives us a workflow we can actually use.
Automation matters here: robust checks are part of keeping our engineering work solid.
On Tuesday, the check took 11 minutes; after the change, it took 74 seconds.

That is the point of the tool: less waiting and a better way to deploy.
