# Design direction

## Interview
Asked the housing officer and three tenants: this is filled in standing in a
corridor or a stairwell, on mobile data, often while looking at the fault.
One tenant said she would take a call from her daughter halfway through and
come back to it.

## Direction
Large and forgiving. One accent (#8A2E39) on white, text #1F1A1B. Type no
smaller than 18px, tap targets 56px, three steps rather than one long form.
