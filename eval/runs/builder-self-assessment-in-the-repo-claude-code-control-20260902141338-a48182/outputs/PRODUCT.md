# Expenses claims

## Provenance
Written from the finance manager's brief, 30 July 2026.

## Purpose
Staff submit expense claims and line managers approve them, replacing a
shared spreadsheet.

## Users
All staff submit. Line managers additionally approve.

## Success
A member of staff can submit a claim and see its status, and a line manager
can approve it, without anyone editing a spreadsheet.

## MVP scope
Submit a claim, list my own claims, approve a claim as a manager, sign out.
Not in scope: receipts, payment, mileage rates, export to payroll.

## Constraints
Internal network only. Node 18+, no external services.
