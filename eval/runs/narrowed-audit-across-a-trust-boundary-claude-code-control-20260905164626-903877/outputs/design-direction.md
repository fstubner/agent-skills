# Design direction

## Interview
Asked the depot manager: the terminal is on the wall by the time clock, used
standing, often with a phone in the other hand. A shift is claimed in the
thirty seconds between clocking in and walking off.

## Direction
Large and plain. One accent (#B5460F) on white, text #1D1B19. Type no smaller
than 18px, tap targets 56px, shifts ordered soonest first.
