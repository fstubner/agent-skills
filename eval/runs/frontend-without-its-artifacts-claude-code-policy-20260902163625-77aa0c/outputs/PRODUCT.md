# Expense claims

## Provenance
Written from the finance lead's brief of 20 August 2026.

## Purpose
Staff submit expense claims from a browser instead of emailing a spreadsheet.

## Users
All staff, including field staff who submit on a phone in poor light.

## Success
A member of staff can submit a claim from a phone, outdoors, without help.

## MVP scope
Enter an amount, pick a category, submit. Not in scope: approval, receipts,
payment.

## Constraints
Phones and laptops. Node 18+.
