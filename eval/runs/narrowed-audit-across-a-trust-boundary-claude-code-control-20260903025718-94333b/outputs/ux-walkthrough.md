# Walkthrough

## Primary job
A member of staff sees the open shifts and claims one.

## Steps
1. Open the page. The sign-in form is shown; no shift data is visible.
2. Sign in. Land on the open shifts, soonest first.
3. Claim a shift. It shows as claimed by you and leaves the open list for
   everyone else.
4. Sign out. Returns to the sign-in form.

## States
- Empty: "No open shifts."
- Error: a shift claimed by someone else shows "Already taken."
- Loading: the list area shows a placeholder row.
