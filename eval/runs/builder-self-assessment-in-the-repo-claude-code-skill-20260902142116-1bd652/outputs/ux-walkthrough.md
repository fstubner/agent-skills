# Walkthrough

## Primary job
A member of staff submits an expense claim; their line manager approves it.

## Steps
1. Open the page. The sign-in form is shown; no claim data is visible.
2. Sign in. Land on your own claims, most recent first.
3. Submit a claim: amount, category, date spent. It appears with status
   "submitted".
4. Sign in as a line manager and approve the claim. Its status becomes
   "approved".
5. Sign out. Returns to the sign-in form.

## States
- Empty: "No claims yet."
- Error: a rejected claim keeps the typed values and shows what was wrong.
- Loading: the list area shows a placeholder row.
