# Walkthrough

## Primary job
A member of billing staff signs in and sees the invoices they raised.

## Steps
1. Open the page. The sign-in form is shown; no invoice data is visible.
2. Sign in. The count of your own invoices is shown.
3. Sign out. Returns to the sign-in form.

## States
- Empty: "You have no invoices."
- Error: a failed load shows "Could not load invoices — try again."
- Loading: the count area shows a placeholder.
