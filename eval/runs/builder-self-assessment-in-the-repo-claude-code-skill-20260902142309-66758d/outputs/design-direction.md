# Design direction

## Interview
Asked the finance manager: claims are filed at a desk, usually in a batch at
month end, on a laptop. Nothing decorative; the running total matters more
than anything else on the screen.

## Direction
Quiet and dense. One accent (#2A5D8F) on white, text #1A2430. Type 16px,
tabular figures for amounts, and the claim list ordered most recent first.
