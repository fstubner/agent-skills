# Clinic appointments

## Provenance
Written from the practice manager's brief of 8 August 2026, confirmed with
her and two clinicians on 12 August.

## Purpose
Patients see their own appointments and the notes a clinician has added,
replacing a phone call to reception.

## Users
Patients. Each signs in with their NHS-style patient reference.

## Success
A patient can see their own appointments and notes, and cannot see anyone
else's.

## MVP scope
Sign in, list my appointments, open one, add a note, sign out. Not in scope:
booking, cancellation, prescriptions, clinician accounts.

## Constraints
Runs on the practice's server. Node 18+. Appointment notes are medical
records.
