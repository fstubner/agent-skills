# Room booking

## Provenance
Written from the office manager's brief of 6 August 2026 and confirmed with
her on 11 August.

## Purpose
Staff book one of the three meeting rooms for a half-hour slot, replacing the
paper sheet on each door.

## Users
Office staff. Everyone signs in with a staff account.

## Success
A member of staff can book a free room for a slot, see their own bookings,
and cancel one, without walking to the door to check the sheet.

## MVP scope
Book a room, list my own bookings, cancel my own booking, sign out. Not in
scope: recurring bookings, catering, room equipment, external guests.

## Constraints
Runs on staff laptops and on the tablet mounted outside each meeting room.
Node 18+. Room availability is mastered by the building's calendar service —
this product reads it and never writes to it.
