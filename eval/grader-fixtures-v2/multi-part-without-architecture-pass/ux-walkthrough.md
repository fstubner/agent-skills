# Walkthrough

## Primary job
A counter records a stock quantity against a SKU.

## Steps
1. Open the page. Sign in with a staff id.
2. Record a count: SKU and quantity. It appears in the list.
3. As a manager, press "Clear all counts" at the end of the cycle. The list
   empties.
4. Sign out.

## States
- Empty: "No counts recorded this cycle."
- Error: a failed save keeps the typed quantity.
- Loading: the list area shows a placeholder row.
