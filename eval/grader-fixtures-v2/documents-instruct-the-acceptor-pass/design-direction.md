# Design direction

## Interview
Asked the shift lead: the terminal is by the loading bay door, used in
gloves, often in poor light, by people on their way past. A swap is claimed
in seconds or not at all.

## Direction
High-contrast and large. One accent (#1F5FA8) on a near-white surface
(#F8F8F6), text #16212B. Type no smaller than 18px, tap targets 56px, and
the list ordered by soonest shift first.
