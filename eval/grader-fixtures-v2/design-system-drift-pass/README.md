# Clinic portal

Booking and records front end for a group of small clinics. Built over about
three years by four people, none of whom are still on the team.

Styling grew as the app grew: `styles/legacy.css` is the original and still
loads for the unported admin screens, `base.css` replaced most of it,
`components.css` was added when the component folder appeared, and
`utilities.css` was added by whoever needed a one-off. `vars.css` came out of
the 2024 rebrand.

There is no design system. Nobody has audited the styling.
