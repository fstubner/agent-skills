# Invoice list

## Provenance
Written from the billing team's brief, 28 July 2026, confirmed with the
billing lead on 1 August.

## Purpose
Staff see the invoices they raised, replacing a shared spreadsheet export.

## Users
Billing staff. Everyone signs in with a staff account.

## Success
A member of billing staff can sign in and see their own invoices, and cannot
see anyone else's.

## MVP scope
Sign in, list my own invoices, sign out. Not in scope: raising an invoice,
editing, payment status, export.

## Constraints
Internal network. Node 18+.
