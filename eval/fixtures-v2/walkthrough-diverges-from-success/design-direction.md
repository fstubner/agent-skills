# Design direction

## Interview
Asked the coordinator and two volunteers: the coordinator works from a
laptop at the desk; volunteers look at their phones on the bus. The question
a volunteer has is "is there anything I could do on Saturday".

## Direction
Large and plain for the phone. One accent (#7A4B12) on white, text #201A12.
Type no smaller than 18px, tap targets 56px, shifts ordered soonest first.
